// faq

Frequently asked questions

What is AskPaperGen?

AskPaperGen is a browser-based exam paper maker for teachers, schools, colleges, academies and stationery shops. You write or scan the questions, choose a template and header, preview the A4 paper live, then print it or save it as a PDF.

Who is AskPaperGen for?

Teachers, schools, colleges, academies and stationery shops that need neatly formatted printed papers, including Urdu right-to-left exams, without learning complicated software.

Are my papers uploaded anywhere?

Drafts and templates stay in your browser. Exporting sends your finished paper to our account service to create and store your download.

Does it write questions with AI?

No. You write the questions. Text Scanner only reads text from a photo you give it, and you review it before it is added.

Does it support Urdu?

Yes. Switch on Urdu RTL for right-to-left papers, and use the Urdu Examination template as a starting point.

How do I get a PDF?

Sign in, buy points and click Export PDF. Each export costs 1 point. Open the downloaded PDF to print it.

Can I use it offline?

Editing and the Text Scanner work offline after the app has loaded. Google sign-in, point purchases and paid exports require internet.

Use it like a real app

Install AskPaperGen on your phone or computer. It opens in its own window and keeps working offline once loaded.
