// faq

Frequently asked questions

Are my papers uploaded anywhere?

No. Papers, drafts and templates are stored in your browser on your own device.

Does it write questions with AI?

No. You write the questions. Scan to Paper only reads text from a photo you give it, and you review it before it is added.

Does it support Urdu?

Yes. Switch on Urdu RTL for right-to-left papers, and use the Urdu Examination template as a starting point.

How do I get a PDF?

Click Export PDF, then choose Save as PDF in your browser print dialog.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Install it as an app and it keeps working without internet once it has loaded.

Use it like a real app

Install AskPaperGen on your phone or computer. It opens in its own window and keeps working offline once loaded.
