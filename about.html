About AskPaperGen

AskPaperGen is a manual exam paper formatting tool for teachers, schools, colleges, academies and stationery shops. It is not an AI question generator — you write the questions, and AskPaperGen makes them look professional.

Everything runs in your browser. Papers and templates are stored on your own device; nothing is uploaded.
