Privacy

Drafts and scanning

Draft papers, templates and OCR processing stay in your browser on your device. Clearing browser storage can remove your drafts.

Accounts and payments

Google sign-in provides your Google account identifier, name and verified email. Our account service stores these details, point balances, payment references, sender names, review decisions and point history. Google operates its own sign-in service.

Exports

Exporting sends your finished paper, including embedded images, to our service. Export records and generated downloads are stored in a private bucket and accessed through authenticated requests. Accounts and exports use Cloudflare services. Sign-in, payments and exports require internet access.

Storage and contact

Session credentials are stored in browser session storage and expire on the server after seven days. Account, payment and export records remain until removed by the service operator. For access, corrections or deletion requests, use our contact page.
